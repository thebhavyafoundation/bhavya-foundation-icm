# Digital Institute

Practical education and capability-building arm of the Knowledge Mission. Broader than AI education.

## Scope
- **Digital Foundations**: computer literacy, internet literacy, cybersecurity awareness, digital productivity, information literacy
- **AI Education**: fundamentals, ML concepts, prompting, verification, responsible AI, AI-assisted creation, AI programming
- **Technology Creation**: coding, websites, automation, data, robotics, software engineering
- **Research Skills**: critical thinking, scientific reasoning, information verification, research methodology, experimentation
- **Mission Projects**: students apply technology to real Forest, Heritage, Community, and Knowledge problems

## Flagship Program
**Rural AI Education for children of Himachal Pradesh**

Pathway:
Digital Literacy → Computational Thinking → AI Literacy → AI Fluency → AI Creation → Real-World Projects → Community Problem Solving

Objective: young people who can understand, question, verify, build with, and recognize the limits of AI — not merely prompt it.

## Delivery Requirements
Low-bandwidth · mobile-friendly · offline-capable · affordable · safe  
Supports schools, rural learning centers, teachers, volunteers, community programs, home learning, shared devices.
