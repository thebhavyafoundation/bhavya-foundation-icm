# Bhavya AI Lab

The experimental and research engine of the Knowledge Mission.

## Scope (Master PRD §5–6)
Investigates:
- Artificial intelligence & machine learning
- Open-source models
- AI safety & responsible AI
- Educational AI
- Local-language AI
- AI for rural communities
- AI for environmental research (Forest)
- AI for heritage preservation
- AI for institutional productivity

AI education is one output. AI applied to Bhavya’s other missions is another.

## Architecture
```
AI LAB
  │
  ├── RESEARCH (papers, benchmarks, evaluations, ethics)
  ├── EXPERIMENTS (prototypes, models, datasets, agents)
  └── APPLICATIONS (mission tools, education, forest, heritage, community)
```

## Outputs
Research, prototypes, datasets, educational material, open-source contributions, internal tools, AI agents, mission-specific applications, public demonstrations.
