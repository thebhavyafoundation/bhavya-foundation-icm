Forest Mission

Restore ecosystems. Protect biodiversity. Increase forest cover. Conserve water.

The Forest Mission is Bhavya’s commitment to environmental stewardship — especially across Himalayan landscapes where forest, water, and community life are inseparable.

Focus areas

  • Native forest restoration and long-term care
  • Water security and watershed health
  • Biodiversity protection
  • Climate resilience for communities and ecosystems

Claims about hectares, survival rates, or impact will be published only when documented. Evidence before claims.
