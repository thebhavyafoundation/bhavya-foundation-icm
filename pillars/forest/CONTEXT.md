# Forest Pillar (Constitutional: Nature)

**Public name**: Forest  
**Constitutional name**: Nature  
**Purpose**: Restore and protect living ecosystems. Secure the future of forests, water, and biodiversity for generations to come.

## Focus Areas (from public design + PRD)
- Reforestation / Native Forest Restoration
- Water Security (rivers, springs, lakes)
- Wildlife & Biodiversity protection
- Sustainable Living
- Climate Action
- Ecological research & environmental education

## Flagship Direction
Green Bhavya Mission and related restoration programmes.  
Work with communities and science to restore forests, revive water bodies, and protect biodiversity.

## Key Metrics (aspirational / design system)
- 230+ Hectares Restored
- 58+ Water Bodies Revived
- 1.2M+ Trees Nurtured
