# Brand System

**Official Name**: Bhavya Foundation  
**Short / Digital Identity**: Bhavya  
**Tagline**: Building for Generations.  
**Pillar line**: Nature. Knowledge. Heritage. (public missions also include Community)

## Logo
Primary logo lives in `assets/logo.png`.  
The mark combines a stylized “B”, living tree, rising sun, mountains, and an open book — Nature + Knowledge + Heritage in one symbol.
