## 1. Executive Product Decision

Bhavya Foundation is not an AI-education company.  
Bhavya Foundation is a long-term institution organized around four missions:

- Forest
- Knowledge
- Heritage
- Community

AI education is a major program inside the Knowledge Mission.  
The technology strategy must support the entire institution rather than allowing one program to consume the architecture.

The system consists of four strategic layers:

```
BHAVYA FOUNDATION
       │
┌──────┼──────┐
│      │      │
MISSIONS  INSTITUTES  INTERNAL OS
│      │      │
Forest AI Lab   GitOS
Knowledge Digital Institute
Heritage Content OS
Community
```

The public-facing institution is Bhavya Foundation.  
The mission layer expresses what Bhavya does.  
The institutes and platforms execute the missions.  
GitOS remains internal infrastructure that helps Bhavya discover, evaluate, integrate, and govern open-source technology, AI skills, code, agents, and harnesses.

## 2. Institutional Vision

Bhavya exists to build institutions and capabilities that endure across generations.  
The four missions are different expressions of one institutional purpose.

**FOREST** — Restore and protect living ecosystems.  
**KNOWLEDGE** — Advance education, science, AI, research, and access to knowledge.  
**HERITAGE** — Preserve culture, history, architecture, traditions, and collective memory.  
**COMMUNITY** — Empower people through education, opportunity, participation, and local development.

These missions share people, knowledge, research, technology, communication, funding, volunteers, evidence, partnerships, and institutional memory.  
Architecture must create shared capabilities, not duplicated applications.

## 3. Product Philosophy

Few strategic products. Deep capabilities. Shared infrastructure.

Mission → Program → Institute / Capability → Content / Research / Project → Public Impact
